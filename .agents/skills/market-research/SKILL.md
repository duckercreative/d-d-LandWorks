# Market Research & ICP Analysis — 75 Degree AC

Research the real Houston HVAC market. Build detailed, research-grounded buyer personas. Every data point must come from actual research — competitor reviews, forums, Google data, not assumptions.

---

## Houston HVAC Market Context

**Known market facts (pre-loaded):**
- Houston: 4th largest US city, ~2.3M city / 7.3M metro population
- Climate: subtropical humid — extreme heat summers, mild winters with occasional cold snaps
- Summer heat index regularly exceeds 110°F (June–September)
- Average Houston home: 2,400 sq ft, built 1978–2005 (heavy AC usage history)
- Homeownership rate: ~43% (below national avg — large rental market also needs HVAC)
- Major HVAC demand drivers: summer emergency calls, pre-summer tune-ups, post-freeze heating repair
- Market maturity: highly competitive — 500+ licensed HVAC contractors in Greater Houston
- Top competitors research at: Google Maps "AC repair Houston", "HVAC Houston"

**Seasonal demand pattern (Houston-specific):**
- March–May: Pre-summer tune-up season (proactive homeowners)
- June–September: PEAK emergency season (AC failures in 100°F+ heat)
- October–November: Pre-winter heating prep (furnace tune-ups)
- December–February: Heating emergencies during cold snaps; low AC demand
