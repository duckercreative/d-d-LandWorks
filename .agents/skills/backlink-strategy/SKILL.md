# Backlink Strategy — 75 Degree AC

## Link Building Principles for Local HVAC

**Quality > Quantity.** 10 links from Houston-relevant, authoritative sites beat 100 generic directory links.

**Priority order:**
1. Local Houston links (highest local ranking impact)
2. Industry association links (topical authority)
3. Manufacturer/brand directories (entity authority)
4. Home services platforms (traffic + citations)
5. Content/blogger outreach (topical authority)

**2025 local SEO link data:**
- Link signals = ~11% of local pack ranking weight (Whitespark 2024)
- Local + relevant links > authority-only links for local SEO
- Sponsored/community links count for local signal even if nofollowed

## Link Tier 4: Content Outreach

### Blog/Content Targets

**Houston home improvement bloggers:**
Search: `site:houstonchronicle.com "ac repair"` — find journalists who cover HVAC topics
Search: `"houston" "hvac" OR "air conditioning" inurl:blog` — find HVAC bloggers

**Outreach angles:**
1. **Expert quote angle:** "Happy to provide an expert quote for your Houston summer survival guide"
2. **Data angle:** "We have interesting data on Houston AC failure rates by season — available for your readers"
3. **Broken link angle:** Find pages linking to defunct HVAC companies, offer our page as replacement
4. **Guest post angle:** Offer a 1,000-word expert article on HVAC topics for their blog

## Anchor Text Strategy

**Distribution target (avoid over-optimization):**

| Anchor Type | Target % | Examples |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Branded | 40% | "75 Degree AC", "75degreeac.com" |
| URL/naked | 25% | "https://75degreeac.com" |
| Partial match | 20% | "Houston HVAC contractor", "AC repair in Houston" |
| Generic | 10% | "click here", "learn more", "website" |
| Exact match | 5% | "ac repair houston tx" (use sparingly) |
