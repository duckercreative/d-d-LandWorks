# GBP Posting — 75 Degree AC

## Why GBP Posts Matter

- GBP posts signal active business → local ranking factor
- Posts appear in local panel → direct conversion opportunity
- Q&A section feeds Google AI Overviews for local queries (confirmed Google I/O 2024)
- Photo engagement → CTR improvement in local pack

**Post frequency benchmark (Houston HVAC top performers):**
- 2–3 posts per week
- Mix of types: What's New, Offer, Event
- Photos on 80%+ of posts
