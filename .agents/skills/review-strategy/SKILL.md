# Review Strategy — 75 Degree AC

## Why Reviews Are Critical

- Review signals = **16–20% of local pack ranking weight** (Whitespark 2026)
- **Review velocity jumped from factor #93 to #11 in 2026** — recency now outweighs total count. 5 fresh reviews in the last 30 days beats 200 stale reviews.
- **Owner response rate of 80%+** triggers a measurable ranking lift (Google has confirmed responding to reviews improves rank)
- Star rating displayed in SERP = direct CTR impact
- **AggregateRating schema** enables star snippets in organic results
- AI systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity) cite review platforms as authority signals
- **Review keywords feed RELEVANCE**, not just prominence. Reviews that mention specific services ("emergency AC repair"), neighborhoods ("Katy"), or products ("mini-split") reinforce ranking for those exact query types.

**Houston HVAC Benchmarks:**
- Top local pack position: 200+ Google reviews, 4.8+ rating, AND fresh reviews in last 30 days
- Winning vs. established competitors requires velocity + recency + response rate + keyword-rich review text
- Review recency weighted 3–5× more than older reviews (last 60–90 days most heavily weighted post-March 2026 core update)
- A profile with 80 reviews + steady weekly flow now outranks one with 200 reviews and nothing in the past 6 months

**Targets:**
- Month 3: 60+ reviews, 4.8+ avg, 80%+ response rate
- Month 6: 120+ reviews, 4.9+ avg, 80%+ response rate
- Velocity: 8–15 new reviews/month (consistent weekly cadence — not batched)
- Response time: within 24 hours for ALL reviews (positive and negative)

**Solicitation tactics that lift keyword relevance:**
Don't just ask for "a review" — ask a question that plants a keyword. After job completion, text: "What did [Tech Name] fix today?" or "How was your emergency AC repair experience in [neighborhood]?" The reviewer's answer naturally contains the query terms Google uses for relevance scoring. Never script or dictate review text (Google's review-spam detection flags coordinated patterns).

### Negative Reviews (1–2 star)

```
[Name], we're sorry to hear your experience wasn't what we'd expect of ourselves. 
We stand behind our work and want to make this right. Please call us directly at 
(713) 598-2737 and ask for [Manager Name] — we'll take care of this for you. 
— 75 Degree AC Team
```

**Critical rules for negative responses:**
- Never be defensive
- Never argue with facts in the review
- Always offer to resolve by phone (not in public comment)
- Never offer discounts/refunds publicly (invites gaming)
- Respond within 24 hours maximum

## Review Content Mining

**What to look for in 5-star reviews:**
- Phrases customers use naturally → use in content + ad copy
- Specific technicians praised → highlight on About page
- Specific services praised → feature in content for those pages
- Urgency stories → use in emergency page messaging

**Example mining:**
Review: "They came out same day and fixed our AC in 2 hours — amazing"
→ Content: "Most repairs completed within 2 hours of arrival"
→ Ad copy: "Same-Day AC Repair — Fixed in Hours, Not Days"

## Competitor Review Monitoring

Check top 3 competitors' Google reviews monthly:
- New review volume (how fast are they growing?)
- Common complaints (opportunities to differentiate)
- Common praise (what customers value — mirror in our messaging)
- Response quality (beat them on response time and professionalism)

## AggregateRating Schema

Once reviews are accumulating, add to homepage and service pages:
```json
{
  "@type": "AggregateRating",
  "ratingValue": "4.9",
  "reviewCount": "87",
  "bestRating": "5",
  "worstRating": "1"
}
```
**Update this monthly** as review count changes. Stale numbers = negative trust signal.
