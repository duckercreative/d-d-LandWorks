## The tree/stump scope-nuance — explicit resolution needed before the brief locks it in

This pack surfaced a real nuance that is not a contradiction but does need explicit confirmation before the content brief treats it as settled:

- **business-info.md's verified service list** does not include "Tree Removal" or "Stump Grinding" as their own line items.
- **The topical map's own scope fence** (§4, §6.4) explicitly excludes tree removal, stump grinding, forestry mulching, and hauling "unless verified."
- **The current live `/land-clearing/` page** already describes felling trees and grubbing stumps as line items of the land-clearing service itself.

**Recommended resolution for the brief stage (not resolved here — flagged for David):** frame land clearing as a whole-parcel service that removes trees and stumps *within its own footprint*, while stating plainly that D&D does not offer standalone single-tree removal, arborist services, or stump grinding as an isolated service independent of a clearing project. This preserves both the live page's existing copy and the topical map's scope discipline without contradiction — but it is an inference this pack is making from the shipped page, not a fact independently confirmed in business-info.md, and should be checked with David directly.
