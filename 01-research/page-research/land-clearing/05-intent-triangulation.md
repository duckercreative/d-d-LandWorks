# Intent Triangulation — Land Clearing (`/land-clearing/`)

No scraped review text (Google/Yelp 1-3 star) was retrievable for any competitor this pass — same limitation disclosed in all four prior packs. Intent triangulation below is built from industry-pattern literature (cost guides, forestry/landowner forum threads via TractorByNet, competitor FAQ content) and Oregon-specific structural facts, not quoted complaints.

---

## Table 1 — Pain Points

| Pain point | Source | Severity | Addressed in current live content? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sticker shock on cost-per-acre once a quote comes in | Industry cost-guide sourcing showing $500-$6,000+/acre range depending on vegetation/terrain | High | No — current live page has zero pricing content |
| Confusion about what happens to cleared trees/brush/debris (hauled? mulched? burned?) | Task brief; industry sourcing on 3 disposal methods | High | Partially — current live page names chip/haul/burn but doesn't explain when each applies or what it costs |
| Fear of accidentally clearing a protected tree or wetland-adjacent area | Eugene/Springfield tree-ordinance research; DSL removal-fill research | Medium-high | No — zero mention of any tree ordinance or wetland rule on the current live page |
| Not knowing whether a permit (tree, burn, grading) is needed before starting | Task brief; multi-agency permit research (Eugene/Springfield/ODF/LRAPA/DEQ) | High | No |
| Worry that "land clearing" and "brush clearing" are the same thing and being oversold on scope | Danco's own FAQ exists specifically because customers ask this; task-brief-named scoping question | Medium | Partially — current live page implies the distinction via its own copy and the sibling page's copy, but never states it head-on as an FAQ |
| Losing a specific tree or landscape feature they wanted kept | Live page's own "Flagging and preserving trees" copy; All Dirt's matching copy | Medium | Yes — both D&D and All Dirt already state this practice, though neither explains the walk-through process in FAQ depth |
| DIY-vs-hire uncertainty for a rural landowner with a tractor/rented excavator | TractorByNet forum threads (DIY Land Clearing, Fair price to clear an acre) | Medium | No |
| Being quoted a flat "we do it all" number with no line-item transparency (stump removal separate? hauling separate?) | Industry sourcing noting stump removal is often billed as an add-on even within a broader clearing job | Medium | No |

## Table 2 — Decision Triggers

| Trigger | Maps to |
|---|---|

## Table 3 — Trust Killers

| Trust killer | Rebuttal to build into the page |
|---|---|
| No pricing signal anywhere for land clearing specifically | Publish real, sourced cost ranges for both construction-grade and pasture/acreage-reclamation tiers, clearly labeled as general estimates, not Lane-County-exact |
| "Land clearing" and "brush clearing" used interchangeably with no clear line | State the real scope difference plainly (trees/stumps/full-parcel = land clearing; brush/scrub/no-timber = brush clearing) and link to the sibling page for the lighter-scope job |
| Permit/regulatory silence — nobody mentions tree ordinances, burn permits, or wetland rules | Add a plain-language permit-awareness section naming which agency governs which situation (Eugene/Springfield tree ordinance vs. ODF/LRAPA/DEQ burning vs. DSL wetlands), with the explicit caveat to confirm specifics directly before clearing |
| Fear that debris disposal is a hidden cost sprung on the customer after the job starts | Explain the three disposal paths (haul/mulch/burn) and their rough cost implications upfront, scoped at the walk-through |
| Worry that a valued tree or feature will be removed without being asked | State plainly, as the current live page and All Dirt both already do, that trees/features can be flagged and preserved — and explain that this happens at the walk-through, before the quote |
| Small residential clearing jobs (a couple acres) feeling like an afterthought next to "subdivision"-scale language | State plainly, matching the homepage pack's established pattern, that a small clearing job and a larger commercial parcel are scoped and quoted the same way |
| Zero reviews/before-after photos at launch | Do not fabricate; be transparent that D&D is a growing local business, consistent with the homepage pack's rule |
