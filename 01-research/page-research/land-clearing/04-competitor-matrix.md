## 5. Summary — the biggest structural opportunity

No competitor in this set combines all of the following on a single land-clearing page: (1) a clear, evidence-based scope line against brush clearing, (2) an explanation of what happens to debris and why the method matters for cost, (3) a plain-language walk-through of which government agency's tree/burn/wetland rule applies to which situation, and (4) real, differentiated pricing for construction-grade versus pasture/acreage-reclamation clearing. D&D's current live page already leads on selective-clearing/preservation framing and disposal-method naming; the content brief's highest-leverage work is adding the regulatory-clarity and pricing-differentiation layers that no one — including Danco and All Dirt, the two strongest competitors in this specific niche — currently provides.
