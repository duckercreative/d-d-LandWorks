## The single biggest content-gap opportunity (headline finding)

**Even the single best piece of Oregon-specific content found anywhere in this research — a dedicated Cojo Asphalt article titled "Frost Depth for Footings: How Deep to Dig in Oregon" — explicitly declines to state Oregon's actual frost-depth figure, deferring entirely to "your local building department."** This page can state the real, verified number (12 inches for Lane County, per ORSC R403.1.4, R403.1.4.1, and Table R301.2) with a genuine code citation. That is a rare, high-confidence finding: it isn't just that no *local* competitor covers this topic (true of nearly every page in this research series) — it's that the best available *national* content on this exact query, from a source clearly trying to cover it well, still pulls the punch on the one number that matters most. Landing that single fact accurately and citably is this page's clearest, lowest-effort, highest-authority win.

## How this page draws its line against the already-shipped Excavation & Grading page (per the task's explicit instruction)

The shipped `/excavation/` page names "foundation dig" as one of five project types under its "What Kind of Project Is This For?" section, with the exact sentence: "Digging and prepping the excavation for a home, shop, barn, or ADU foundation, following site preparation on the same lot. See foundation excavation for more depth on this project type." That sentence is a promise this page must actually keep. The boundary:

**Stays on `/excavation/` (do not re-derive here):**

**Belongs here, genuinely new to this page:**
- The actual Oregon/Lane County frost-line depth figure and its code citation
- The slab vs. crawlspace vs. basement foundation-type excavation-scope and cost distinction
- The specific OSHA house-foundation/basement over-dig/working-space figure (2 ft minimum) — a different, more specific citation than the general Subpart P figures already used on the excavation page
- The excavate → inspect → pour sequencing with Eugene/Lane County's actual building-department process
- The foundation drain (ORSC R405) and crawlspace vapor-retarder (R408) code requirements
- ADU-specific foundation excavation content, including the OSHA underpinning rule for digging near an existing structure
- The higher-stakes framing of rock/water discovery specifically because a foundation dig sits on a construction critical path

This is the same kind of real, defensible distinction the excavation pack drew against the future Grading & Leveling page and the site-preparation pack drew against the excavation page itself — not an arbitrary keyword split, but a genuine difference in what each page's content is actually for.
