# Intent Triangulation — Foundation Excavation (`/foundation-excavation/`)

Same three-table approach as the six prior packs. This page's intent profile is meaningfully different from its siblings: a foundation excavation buyer is almost never excavating in isolation. They're nearly always tied to an active building permit, a builder or GC's schedule, and an inspection sequence they don't control — which raises the stakes on schedule reliability and cost-anxiety well above a standalone driveway cut, pond dig, or yard regrade.

## Why this page's intent profile differs from its five siblings

Every prior pack in this series (homepage, site prep, excavation, grading-leveling, land clearing, septic) deals with a buyer making a relatively self-contained decision: clear my lot, level my yard, dig my pond, fix my septic. Foundation excavation is structurally different — it is nearly always a **dependent step inside someone else's larger project**, whether that's a homeowner's build managed by a GC, a builder's spec-home schedule, or a commercial project's permit timeline. That changes what "trust" means here: it's less about "will you show up and do good work" (still true, but table stakes) and more about "will you understand and respect the schedule and inspection process you're plugging into, and will you tell me the real numbers instead of a vague reassurance." The content plan above is built around that distinction, not a re-skin of the excavation page's general trust-signal content.
