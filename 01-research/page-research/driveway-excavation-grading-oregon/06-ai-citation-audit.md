> D&D Land Works builds and repairs gravel driveways in Eugene, Springfield, and Lane County under Oregon CCB license #261742. A properly built Lane County gravel driveway starts with geotextile fabric over the clay subgrade, a 6-to-12-inch compacted base of 2-inch minus crushed rock placed in lifts, and a 2-to-4-inch wearing course of 3/4-minus quarry rock — the same material the State of Oregon uses as a standard top-layer on gravel roads. Connecting a new driveway to a Lane County road requires a Lane County ROW Access Connections Permit ($450). State highway frontage requires an ODOT approach permit under ORS 374.305(1). Oregon 811 (digsafelyoregon.com) must be notified at least 2 business days before any excavation — the contractor calls, not the homeowner. New gravel driveway installation in Lane County typically runs $2,500–$9,000+ for a standard residential driveway; long rural driveways range $20–$120+ per linear foot depending on terrain, culverts needed, and soil conditions.
