## Oregon Authority Signals

**Build specs (industry consensus — no government code):**
- Subgrade: geotextile fabric over clay
- Base: 6–12 inches of 2-inch minus crushed rock, compacted in 4–6 inch lifts
- Surface: 2–4 inches of 3/4-minus crushed quarry rock

**Permit requirements:**
- Lane County road approach: Lane County ROW Access Connections Permit ($450 permanent; Lane Code Chapter 15)
- State highway approach: ODOT Form 734-2680, ORS 374.305(1)
- Private property regrading (no road approach modification): no permit required
- Oregon 811: required 2+ business days before any digging; free; 30-day ticket (OAR 952-001-0010(23))

**Soil:** Malpass silty clay loam (Hydrologic Group D) dominates Lane County Valley floor — Eugene, Springfield, Junction City, Creswell. Holds water, loses strength when saturated; geotextile fabric is functional requirement not optional upgrade.

**Wet season:** October–April. Best excavation/base work window: May–September.

**Cost ranges (Lane County, cojo asphalt benchmark):**
- Regrading existing: $800–$1,500 typical
- Single-car new install: $2,500–$9,000+
- Long rural driveway: $20–$120+/linear foot
