1. **Frost depth + pricing + inspection contacts** (scores 50.0 each) — Three claims no local competitor makes anywhere on any page. Zero-cost to include. Cojo Asphalt ranks #3 with Oregon content but declines to give the frost depth number. D&D will be the first local Eugene contractor with all three on a foundation excavation page.

2. **Eugene soil context + foundation type comparison** (scores 40.0 each) — Malpass clay is a BLM-documented local phenomenon; crawl space prevalence data comes from a ranking realtor blog. Zero excavation contractors cover either. These are the two anchors of the page's "local knowledge" section.

3. **ADU foundation excavation** (score 40.0) — Oregon's ADU surge generates real residential demand; the query "ADU foundation excavation Eugene" returns no relevant local contractor content. This section captures a growing, uncontested query cluster with high commercial value.

4. **AI Overview citation block + FAQPage schema** (scores 36.0 / 25.0) — AI Overview fires for this query; no local contractor is structured to be cited. FAQPage schema is implemented by zero local competitors. These two technical elements are the path to the #1 effective SERP position (above organic).

5. **Cojo Asphalt displacement** (score 25.0) — A Portland-area contractor blog ranks #3 in Eugene organically. D&D's page — a local contractor with Eugene CCB and specific local content — will have greater geographic authority and displace it within 3–6 months of publish.
