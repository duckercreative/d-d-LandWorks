## FAQ Shortlist — 10 Questions for FAQPage Schema

Validated against SERP competitor analysis (0/10 competitors have FAQ content on foundation-excavation-specific topics) and ranked by search signal strength + Eugene-specific differentiation value:

1. **How deep do footings need to be in Eugene, Oregon?** — 12 inches minimum below undisturbed grade (ORSC R403.1.4); same as the frost line for Lane County's elevation (ORSC Table R301.2)
2. **What is the frost line depth in Lane County, Oregon?** — 12 inches (same answer as Q1 for Lane County's elevation band; cite ORSC Table R301.2)
3. **Does foundation type change what excavation involves?** — Yes, significantly: slab = shallow strip, crawl space = perimeter trench to footing depth, full basement = 8–10 ft excavation with shoring/benching
4. **What foundation type is most common in Eugene?** — Crawl space is most common in Eugene and Lane County (confirmed by local real estate data); slab-on-grade is second most common for newer construction
5. **How does Willamette Valley clay affect excavation for a foundation?** — Malpass clay in west Eugene has high shrink/swell properties and low permeability; saturated clay in winter may require over-excavation to reach firm bearing soil regardless of code minimum depth
6. **Can you excavate for a foundation during Oregon's rainy season?** — Yes, with proper erosion and drainage control; dry season (May–September) is ideal; October–April adds erosion control cost and requires care with compaction
7. **Does the building department inspect a foundation before concrete is poured?** — Yes: in Eugene/Lane County, the footing inspection happens after excavation, forms, and rebar are in place — and before concrete is placed. Lane County Building Safety: (541) 682-4651; schedule 48 hours in advance
8. **Do you call 811 before every dig?** — Yes. Oregon law requires at least 2 business days' advance notice to Oregon 811 before any excavation begins. D&D calls on every job.
9. **What does foundation excavation cost in Eugene, Oregon?** — National ranges: $4–$27/sq ft for slab, $5–$16/sq ft for crawl space, $10–$20/sq ft for full basement (new construction). Eugene jobs vary by soil condition, access, and season. Free estimates available: 541-401-8726.
10. **Do you excavate for ADU foundations in Eugene?** — Yes. Eugene ADU foundations must meet Oregon Residential Specialty Code requirements. Eugene allows ADUs under ORS 197.312; the foundation requirements depend on the ADU type. Call for a site-specific estimate.
