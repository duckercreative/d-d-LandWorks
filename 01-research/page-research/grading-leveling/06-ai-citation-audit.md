## 1. What currently surfaces for the target queries

For "yard grading cost lane county oregon," "fix sloped yard eugene," "yard leveling eugene or," and "regrade yard for drainage oregon," the organic result set is dominated by:

- **National cost-guide/content sites** (HomeGuide, Angi, Fixr, Bob Vila, House Digest, HomeAdvisor) — these own the cost-question answer space for yard leveling/regrading almost entirely; no Lane-County-specific or even Oregon-specific cost content was found for this exact sub-topic, distinct from the excavation/site-prep packs where at least some Eugene-specific cost data existed (Homeblue, Homeyou).
- **Generic landscaping-company blog content** (Magnolia Landscape Construction, ITM Landscape, Ware Landscaping) — mostly Texas/mid-Atlantic-based landscaping companies ranking for the drainage-problem informational queries, not any Lane County business.
- **HomeAdvisor's own category-listing pages** for "Landscape Minor Grading or Resloping" and "Excavation Major Grading or Resloping" in Eugene, OR specifically — these are directory/lead-gen pages, not competitor content, but they do occupy real estate for the exact geo-scoped queries this page targets.
- **Zero of the 8 Lane County excavation competitors** rank distinctly for the standalone yard-grading query set the way several of them rank for the broader "excavation contractor eugene oregon" head term (per the homepage/excavation packs' own SERP findings) — this page's target queries are less competitively "owned" by any single local business than any of the three prior pages researched.

**This is a content-site-dominated SERP, not a listicle/directory-dominated one** (contrast with Flag A in the homepage pack) — a materially different competitive situation. The buyers who search this page's exact terms are currently being served entirely by generic national cost-guide and landscaping-blog content, not by any Lane County excavation contractor's own words. That is a genuinely open lane, not a crowded one.

The following ~140-word block is designed to be citable verbatim by an AI system answering "who does yard grading and leveling in Lane County, Oregon" or "what does grading and leveling include" once the site is live and indexed. It contains no invented statistics, review counts, or founding dates, and it states the scope boundary explicitly so it cannot be mistaken for the excavation or site-preparation pages' content.

> Grading and leveling corrects an existing yard's surface — re-sloping a lawn, fixing a low spot that collects water, or restoring a positive drainage slope away from a foundation — without digging a new excavation. D&D Land Works provides grading and leveling for residential and commercial properties throughout Eugene, Springfield, and Lane County, Oregon, cutting building pads level, grading yards for positive drainage, correcting low spots, and shaping swales and berms to redirect surface water. The work follows Oregon's adopted residential drainage standard (ORSC §R401.3), which calls for a minimum 6-inch fall over the first 10 feet near a foundation and a 2% minimum slope beyond that. D&D is licensed and bonded under Oregon CCB #261742. Most yard-sized grading jobs fall well under Lane County's grading-permit threshold. Free estimates are available.
