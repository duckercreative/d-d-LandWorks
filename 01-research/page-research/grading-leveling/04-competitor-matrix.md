# Competitor Matrix — D&D Land Works Grading & Leveling (`/grading-leveling/`)

Same 8-competitor set the homepage pack established (Danco, All Dirt, Heavy D, Emerald, McKenzie, Braun, Strasheim, A&D), re-checked specifically for **standalone yard grading/leveling content** — i.e., grading offered or explained as its own service for an existing yard, separate from grading tied to a new dig or new construction — rather than rediscovered from scratch. This mirrors the site-preparation and excavation packs' re-check methodology.

**Method note:** `WebFetch` was used directly against All Dirt's excavation service page (the set's strongest, most detailed competitor per all three prior packs); the remaining 7 were re-checked via targeted `WebSearch` queries combining each business name with "yard leveling," "lawn grading," and "regrading" terms, since a full re-fetch of all 8 homepages specifically hunting for a standalone-grading sub-page was not warranted given the consistent pattern found (see below). No competitor was found to publish a dedicated standalone yard-leveling/regrading page or section distinct from general excavation/site-prep copy.

---

## Direct-Check Finding: All Dirt, Inc. (the set's strongest competitor)

Fetched `alldirtinc.com/services/excavation` directly, 2026-09-08. Result: "precision grading" appears as one line item under "Our Excavation Capabilities" — explicitly contextualized to foundation work, site preparation, and new construction. **No standalone yard-leveling, lawn-regrading, or re-sloping content exists anywhere on the page.** This is the same pattern already found for excavation-vs-site-prep disambiguation in the two prior packs: All Dirt names the individual process term (grading) but does not carve out an existing-yard-problem use case as its own topic.

## Matrix B — Third-Party Market Corroboration (new finding for this page)

Unlike the homepage/site-prep/excavation packs, this page has a genuine **third-party taxonomy corroboration point** that those pages didn't have: HomeAdvisor's own lead-generation category system independently splits **"Landscape Minor Grading or Resloping"** (a dedicated Eugene, OR category page exists) from **"Excavation Major Grading or Resloping"** (Eugene/Oregon City category pages exist, and the latter is already cited in the topical map's own source list from earlier research). This means the market's own directory infrastructure already treats "small yard regrade" and "major excavation-scale grading" as genuinely different lead categories — this project isn't inventing an artificial split, it's building content around a distinction the market already prices and routes separately. See `03-entity-map.md` §4.

## Individual Competitor Notes (grading/leveling-specific findings only — see sibling packs for each competitor's full profile)

**All Dirt, Inc.** — Directly confirmed 2026-09-08: "precision grading" listed only within excavation capabilities, tied to foundation/new-construction context. Still "the model to beat" generally (per all three prior packs), but even All Dirt has not claimed the standalone-yard-grading ground this page targets.

**Danco Excavation** — Broadest general service list of the set (also offers tree/stump/hauling, not verified for D&D); grading appears as one word in that list, no yard-specific framing.

**Heavy D Excavation** — Positions "Defensible Space"/wildfire-mitigation framing (noted in the homepage pack) but nothing on standalone yard grading.

**Emerald Excavating** — Longest-tenured competitor (1962); "all phases of site work preparation" language stays construction-framed.

**McKenzie Excavating** — Only competitor with a visible BBB A+ rating (per homepage pack); grading is one function within a general "Excavating" page, not a standalone offering.

**Braun Excavating** — Construction/subdivision-framed language ("home pads," "utility trenching," "subdivisions"); no yard-scale framing found.

**Strasheim Contracting** — Smallest, most locally-scoped site of the set; grading listed alongside brush clearing/site prep/road construction without distinguishing standalone use cases.

**A&D Excavation and Land Improvement** — Broadest "land improvement" positioning of the original 8; not deep-dived for this specific sub-topic this pass, flagged for a future refresh if it surfaces directly in more SERP checks.
