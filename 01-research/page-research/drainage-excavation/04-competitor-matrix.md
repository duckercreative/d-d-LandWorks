# Competitor Matrix — D&D Land Works Drainage Excavation

The core 8-competitor set (Danco, All Dirt, Heavy D, Emerald, McKenzie, Braun, Strasheim, A&D) was re-checked specifically for **buried-drainage-system content** (French drains, curtain drains, catch basins, dry wells, culverts) rather than general "drainage" line items, which every prior pack already confirmed are near-universal but undefined. Three of the eight (Danco, All Dirt, Heavy D) were direct-fetched again on 2026-09-09 for this specific angle. Two **newly-discovered** competitors emerged from drainage-specific searches and are added to this pack's set since they materially change the competitive picture for this exact page: **M4 Landworks** and **Pacific Earthworks LLC**. A contractor-content source, **Cojo Asphalt**, is included for its sourcing value (Oregon-specific French drain trench specs and Springfield storm-drain permit content) but is not treated as a direct local competitor.

## Individual Notes

**M4 Landworks** — The most direct drainage-specific competitor found in this research. Names French drains, culverts, and swales/dry creek beds by name and makes a real longevity claim (15-25+ years). Notably absent: pricing, permits, catch basins, dry wells, material specificity, and any Oregon-specific regulatory content (storm-drain tie-in, DSL). A genuinely closer benchmark to clear than the core 8, but still clearable on every dimension above.

**Pacific Earthworks LLC** — Has a dedicated `/french-drains` URL, itself a signal that this exact service deserves its own page rather than a line item — validates this page's existence as a standalone URL per the topical map. Not deep-dived beyond discovery in this pass; a future refresh should fetch and score it directly if it continues to rank.

**Danco / All Dirt / Heavy D** — Re-confirmed (direct fetch, 2026-09-09) to treat "drainage" as an undefined, generic line item with zero buried-system terminology anywhere. No change from the general "drainage" finding already logged in the home and grading-leveling packs.

**Cojo Asphalt** — Not a competing excavation business (appears to be a paving/asphalt-adjacent content operation reused across multiple Oregon geo-targeted blog posts), but its content is the single best-sourced material found in this entire research pass for real depth/slope/material/cost/permit specifics. Content-source, not competitor; cited accordingly in `07-oregon-authority.md`.
