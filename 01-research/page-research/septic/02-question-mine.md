# Question Mine — Septic Install & Repair (`/services/septic-install-repairs/`)

**Page:** Septic Installation & Repair — the sixth page through this pipeline, and the first genuinely state-regulated, health/safety-critical service page (site prep, excavation, grading, and land clearing are all civil/earthwork; septic is a licensed environmental-health discipline layered on top of earthwork).
**Sourced via:** WebSearch against Google results (PAA-adjacent), `site:quora.com` search-result titles (exact question phrasing), Oregon DEQ / Lane County government pages, septic-industry cost/education sites (Angi, HomeAdvisor, septictankhub, Alpha Environmental, HouseMaster), and the existing homepage/site-preparation question mines cross-referenced for septic-adjacent items already surfaced (reused, not re-derived, where noted).
**Methodology note:** Direct `WebFetch` of reddit.com threads was unreliable this session (bot walls), consistent with every prior pack in this project. Questions marked `reddit_or_quora_sourced` come from verbatim `site:quora.com` search-result question titles or from Reddit-style recommendation-ask phrasing modeled on the confirmed pattern already documented in the homepage pack's own question mine (`../home/02-question-mine.md`) and its explicit "reported honestly rather than papered over with invented quotes" precedent. No fabricated questions.

**Distressed-buyer-tagged (new category for this page — see note below):** 9

**New category for this page — `distressed_buyer`:** Septic is frequently a crisis purchase (system actively failing, sewage backing up, or a home sale contingent on a septic inspection passing) rather than a planned home-improvement decision the way grading or land clearing usually is. The parent homepage/site-prep packs didn't need this tag; this page does, per the task's explicit instruction to surface pain points specific to septic buyers.

## cost

9. How much does a septic system installation cost in Eugene, Oregon? *(answered: average $4,173-$5,963, range $1,487-$10,464 based on 350 completed Eugene-area projects — Homeyou data, reused from home pack)*
10. How much does a septic system cost nationally? *(answered: $3,593-$12,463 typical, national average ~$8,017 — Angi 2026 data)*
11. What does a conventional septic system cost in Oregon? *(answered: roughly $3,000-$10,000 installed — septictankhub 2026 data)*
12. What does an alternative treatment system (ATT) cost in Oregon? *(answered: roughly $12,000-$30,000+ installed — septictankhub 2026 data; Oregon/coastal-climate systems commonly land at the higher end, $18,000-$25,000)*
13. How much does a sand filter septic system cost? *(answered: intermittent sand filter $7,000-$18,000; recirculating sand filter $10,000-$25,000+ — septictankhub 2026 data)*
14. How much does an aerobic treatment unit (ATU) cost? *(answered: $10,000-$20,000 installed, roughly 2-3x a conventional system, plus an ongoing monthly service-contract cost a conventional system doesn't carry — septictankhub 2026 data)*
15. Why is an alternative system so much more expensive than a conventional one? *(decision-stage — directly ties to Gap #1 in `08-gap-analysis.md`)*
16. How much does it cost to repair a septic tank? *(answered: average $1,828, range $631-$3,026; major repairs can run $6,000+ — HomeAdvisor 2026 data)*
17. How much does leach field/drainfield repair or replacement cost? *(answered: $2,000-$20,000 — a genuinely wide range that needs on-page context about what drives it, not just a number)*
18. How much does a septic pump repair or replacement cost? *(answered: roughly $250-$400 for pump repair — HomeAdvisor data)*
19. How much does it cost to fix a failed septic system? *(quora-sourced: "How much does it cost to fix a failed septic system?")*
20. How much does it cost to replace a septic tank? *(quora-sourced: "How much does it cost to replace a septic tank?")*

23. Why do septic quotes vary so much between contractors for what sounds like the same job? *(objection-adjacent — soil type, system type, and site access all change the number significantly)*

## process

24. What happens during a septic site evaluation? *(answered: minimum 2 test pits, 3 preferred, 50-100 feet apart, each 2ft wide x 4ft long x 5ft deep — reused directly from `../site-preparation/07-oregon-authority.md` §5, do not re-derive)*
25. How do I get a septic permit in Lane County? *(answered: site evaluation approval first, then apply to the Subsurface Sanitation Program for the septic permit, often alongside the building permit application — Lane County Land Management Division process)*
26. Does DEQ or Lane County actually issue the permit? *(directly answers the task's core regulatory question — see `07-oregon-authority.md` §1: Lane County is one of DEQ's 33 contract/agent counties, not one of the 3 DEQ directly manages)*
27. How long does the whole septic permit and installation process take? *(answered: physical installation itself often 3-7 days once permitted; the full process including site evaluation, design, and permit approval can run several weeks to a few months)*
28. What's the order of operations — site evaluation, then permit, then building permit, or some other order? *(answered per Lane County's own published sequence: site evaluation → septic permit application (often alongside building permit) → planning clearance → construction)*
29. Does site prep need to happen before or after the septic evaluation? *(reused directly from `../site-preparation/content` and its own FAQ — do not re-derive: the drainfield area must be evaluated and flagged before general grading disturbs it)*
30. What happens if grading disturbs the area that was evaluated for my drainfield? *(answered: it can void the site's septic approval outright — reused directly from `../site-preparation/07-oregon-authority.md` §5)*
31. Do I need to be present for the site evaluation or the installation?
32. Who pulls the septic permit — the homeowner or the contractor? *(process crossover — Lane County's own guidance notes it's "generally advisable" to use a licensed installer but a homeowner may do the work themselves)*
33. What does cleanup and site restoration look like after a septic installation?
