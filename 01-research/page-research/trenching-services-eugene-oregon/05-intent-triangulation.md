## Scenario Layer (5 real-life situations behind these searches)

**Scenario 1 — Irrigation system expansion, south Eugene homeowner:**
Homeowner has an existing in-ground sprinkler system covering the front yard. They've added a raised bed garden in the backyard and want to extend the system 80 feet to include a drip irrigation zone. They're searching "irrigation trenching Eugene OR" to find someone to dig the trench. They want to know: how deep does it need to go, will it damage their existing lawn, can the contractor do it in one visit, and what it will cost for 80 feet.

**Scenario 2 — Rural Lane County property with drainage ditch needs:**
Property owner on a 5-acre lot east of Eugene has standing water on the low side of their driveway. A neighbor told them they need a "perimeter ditch" or French drain. They searched "ditch digging Lane County" and are evaluating whether to hire a professional or rent a trencher themselves. They don't know the difference between a drainage ditch and a French drain — they just know water is pooling.

**Scenario 3 — New detached structure (shop/barn), all utilities needed:**
Springfield homeowner building a detached 30x40 shop needs to run power (200A sub-panel), water, and possibly gas 75 feet from the main house. They've already got an electrician lined up for the connection. They need a trenching contractor who can do the actual trench — ideally all three utilities in one trench to save on cost. They searched "trench digging contractor Lane County."

**Scenario 4 — Broken water line in the front yard:**
Eugene homeowner woke up to a wet patch in the front lawn. A plumber confirmed the water service line is broken 3 feet underground. The plumber handles the pipe repair; they need an excavation contractor to dig the access trench, then backfill after. They searched "emergency trench digging Eugene" at 9am on a weekday. Timeline matters.

**Scenario 5 — ADU utility connections in Eugene:**
Eugene homeowner building a 600 sq ft backyard ADU under Oregon's ADU laws (SB 1051/HB 2001). The contractor quoted the structure; they need a separate excavation contractor for the utility run from the main house. They searched "utility trenching Springfield OR" (they're actually in Eugene but near the Springfield border). They know what they need; they want a licensed, CCB-listed contractor.
