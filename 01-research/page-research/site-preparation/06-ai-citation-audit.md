## 1. What currently surfaces for "site preparation cost lane county oregon" and adjacent queries

For queries run during this pass ("site preparation cost per acre Oregon," "site preparation eugene oregon new construction lot," "building pad preparation what's included cost"), the organic result set is dominated by:

- **National cost-guide/content sites** (Angi, HomeGuide, Saga Infrastructure, TrueBuiltHome, Homewyse) — these own the "how much does site prep cost" answer space almost entirely; no Lane County-specific or Oregon-specific contractor page ranks for the cost-modified query at all.
- **Individual competitor pages**, when the query is scoped to "eugene oregon" specifically — All Dirt's dedicated site-prep page, BBC Excavation's Eugene site-prep page, and Emerald Excavating's homepage all surfaced directly for the geo-scoped query.
- **General "what is site preparation" definitional content** (Quora, Shilling Excavation's blog, Lawinsider's contract-clause glossary) for the bare definitional query — not a single contractor "owns" the definitional answer either.

This is a **narrower, less directory-dominated SERP than the homepage's head term** — directories (Angi/Yelp/BBB) show up for the generic cost query but not for the geo-scoped "site preparation eugene oregon" query, where actual Lane County contractor pages (All Dirt, BBC, Emerald) rank directly. **Implication:** a well-built `/site-preparation/` page has a more direct path to organic and AI-citation visibility here than the homepage does for its own head term — the competitive bar is a handful of real contractor pages, not a wall of aggregators.

## 4. AI-citable summary block draft (110-150 words, site-prep-specific)

> Site preparation is the work that gets a raw or partially-cleared lot ready for construction: clearing brush and debris, stripping and stockpiling topsoil, cutting and filling to bring the pad to design grade, compacting the subgrade, and staking corners and elevations for the build crew. It is distinct from excavation, which is the specific digging done for a foundation, utility line, or drainage feature once the site is prepped. D&D Land Works provides site preparation for residential and commercial projects throughout Eugene, Springfield, and Lane County, Oregon — including new home builds, ADUs, shop and barn pads, subdivisions, and commercial site development — as part of a licensed, bonded (Oregon CCB #261742) full-service excavation company. Site work in Lane County may require a grading permit, an erosion-control permit, or Oregon DEQ's 1200-C stormwater permit depending on project size; D&D can help confirm what applies before work begins.
