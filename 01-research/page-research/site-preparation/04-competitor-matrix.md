## Matrix B — Does the Homepage Pack's "0/8 Structural Depth" Finding Hold for Site Prep Specifically?

**Yes, confirmed, and slightly sharper.** The homepage pack found zero of 8 competitors run a real FAQ section, publish pricing, or provide permit guidance *anywhere on their sites*. Narrowing the lens to site-preparation content specifically across 9 competitors (8 original + BBC Excavation) finds the same zero-coverage pattern holds for FAQ, pricing, and permit guidance — and adds one more specific gap unique to this page: **not one of the 9 competitors explains the difference between "site preparation" and "excavation" as services**, even though several (Danco, Braun, Strasheim) list the words next to each other in the same sentence without distinguishing them. This directly validates the topical map's framing of this page as the "umbrella hub" — nobody currently does the hub job of disambiguating the service group for a confused homeowner.

## Individual Notes Specific to Site Prep

**All Dirt, Inc.** — Still "the model to beat," and more clearly so on this specific page than the homepage-level comparison suggested: its `/services/building-site-prep` URL is a real, if thin, content asset. The gap between All Dirt's page and a genuinely complete site-prep page (FAQ, pricing, permits, process sequence, project types) is large and entirely fillable.

**BBC Excavation Inc** — The most interesting single find of this pass. Being the only competitor (in either the original 8 or this expanded 9) to mention land surveys, soil testing, and geotechnical investigation on a site-prep page suggests this is a real, if rare, point of differentiation in the Lane County market — and one D&D could adopt honestly if David's process includes any soil/survey coordination (confirm with David; do not claim geotechnical services D&D does not actually provide or coordinate).

**McKenzie, Danco, Heavy D, Braun, Strasheim, Emerald, A&D** — Collectively confirm the homepage pack's finding that "site prep" is treated as a synonym for or subset of general excavation across most of this market, not a distinct, fully-scoped service with its own content. This is consistent with, and reinforces, the topical map's instruction that this page function as a disambiguating umbrella hub.
