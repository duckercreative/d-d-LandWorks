## Table 2 — Decision Triggers

| Trigger | Context | Maps to |
|---|---|---|
| Builder/GC needs a subcontractor to dig footings/foundation for a scheduled framing start | B2B/developer trigger, time-sensitive | Homepage/Excavation → Foundation Excavation |

| Trust killer | Why it matters here | D&D rebuttal (explicit, evidence-based — no invented stats) |
