# AI Citation Audit — Excavation & Grading (`/excavation/`)

**Method note:** Same limitation as the homepage and site-preparation packs — this session cannot directly query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude.ai, or Bing Copilot chat interfaces. This audit reasons from (a) what Google's organic/SERP results surface for the target queries, a reliable proxy for what retrieval-augmented AI answers draw from, and (b) documented citation-source patterns for local-service queries. The core finding is verified directly, not assumed: **zero search results across this pack's research surfaced "D&D Land Works," "ddlandworks.com," or "David Deggelman" in any context**, consistent with business-info.md's own finding and the two prior packs' audits.

---

## 1. What currently surfaces for the target queries

For "excavation contractor eugene oregon," "excavation contractor lane county or," and "excavation contractor near me" (Lane County-scoped), the organic result set is dominated by:

- **Directory/listicle aggregators**: DownToBid, BBB, Angi, Yelp, YellowPages, HomeAdvisor — occupying roughly half or more of page-1 positions, consistent with the homepage pack's Flag A finding
- **Individual competitor homepages/service pages**: All Dirt, Danco, Heavy D, Emerald, McKenzie, Braun, Strasheim, A&D, plus **Pacific Excavation, Tectonic Construction, Lane Excavating, Gilmore Excavation, Miles Excavation, Davis Construction and Excavation, B&T Excavation, Crover's Dirt Works, S4 Excavation, and Superior Underground** — a noticeably larger visible competitor set for the bare head term than either the homepage or site-preparation packs found, since "excavation contractor" is the single highest-volume, least-qualified query in this niche (confirming the topical map's own framing of this as the highest-volume head term)
- **Cost-guide content sites** (Angi, HomeGuide, CostFlowAI, Rockzone Americas) dominating cost-modified queries specifically — CostFlowAI's Oregon-specific page (BLS-sourced labor data) is a new, more authoritative cost-guide entrant than what the homepage/site-prep packs found

**This head term has a visibly larger and more fragmented competitor field than the homepage's own target keyword or the site-preparation page's narrower query set** — a materially different SERP shape that matters for strategy: no single competitor (not even All Dirt) commands enough structural depth to be the sole "model to beat" the way it functionally was for site preparation. This is, if anything, a bigger open-field opportunity, not a smaller one — see `08-gap-analysis.md`.

**Is D&D Land Works mentioned anywhere, by any indexed source, for any excavation-specific query tested?** No. Zero mentions, consistent with the homepage and site-preparation packs' own confirmed "total gap" findings — not a data-collection failure, a real and expected finding for a pre-launch business.

**Entity-signal repair implications** (linking to `03-entity-map.md`), same foundational dependencies as the prior two packs, restated briefly rather than re-derived: a live indexed website and a Google Business Profile remain the two highest-leverage prerequisites; directory claims (BBB, Angi, Yelp) are free and currently unclaimed; reviews accumulate over time and should not be fabricated or rushed.
