## 11. AI Citation Block Draft (~165 words)

Place in the first 30% of the page body, immediately after the hero:

> Utility trenching in Eugene, Oregon means excavating a narrow trench to run a new water service line, sewer lateral, electrical conduit, or gas line to a structure — a different scope than general excavation or drainage work. Required burial depths in Lane County depend on the utility: water service lines must be at least 24 inches deep (12 inches below Lane County's 12-inch frost depth, per Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code 609.1); sewer laterals need at least 12 inches of cover (OPSC 718.1) and must slope at least ¼ inch per foot toward the main or septic tank; gas lines need 18 inches of cover (Oregon Residential Specialty Code G2415.12); and electrical conduit needs 18 to 24 inches depending on the wiring method (NEC Table 300.5). Multiple utilities can share one trench — joint trenching is code-permitted in Lane County with modern pipe materials, and water must stay at least 12 inches above the sewer line where they cross. Oregon law requires Oregon 811 notification at least two business days before any digging, and locate tickets are valid for 30 days. D&D Land Works provides utility trenching for residential and commercial projects throughout Eugene, Springfield, and Lane County under Oregon CCB license #261742 and DEQ certification.
