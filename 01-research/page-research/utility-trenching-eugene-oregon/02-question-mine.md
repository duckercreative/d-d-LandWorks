**Oregon811 current facts (verified, for page use):**
- Both homeowners and contractors are legally required to notify
- At least 2 business days advance notice required
- Locate ticket NOW expires after **30 days** (reduced from 45 days in 2025/2026 under OAR 952-001-0010(23) amendment) — no competitor has noted this change
- 811 covers public utility lines to the meter only; private lines are owner's responsibility
- Hand-dig required within 2 feet of marked lines
