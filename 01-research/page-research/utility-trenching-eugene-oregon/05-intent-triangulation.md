## Eugene / Lane County Seasonal Context

**Willamette Valley wet season (October–April):**
- Eugene receives approximately 47" of annual rainfall, heavily concentrated October through April
- Lane County's valley floor has significant clay content (Malpass silty clay loam) that holds water and becomes extremely difficult to excavate when saturated
- Wet-season trenching is possible but may require dewatering, can compromise compaction quality, and may delay inspection scheduling
- **Best time to schedule utility trenching in Lane County:** May through September (dry season window)
- This seasonal context is a legitimate planning consideration no competitor mentions

**Implication for content:** Add a brief "best time to schedule" note in the process section. This is an honest, locally-specific piece of advice that no other page provides, and it drives earlier call-in conversions ("schedule now before the wet season starts").
