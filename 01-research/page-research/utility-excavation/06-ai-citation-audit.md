## 3. Citation Gap List

- "Sewer laterals need at least 12 inches of cover per OPSC 718.1, sloped at least 1/4 inch per foot."
- "Underground gas piping needs at least 18 inches of cover under Oregon's fuel gas code (ORSC G2415.12), or as little as 8 inches for an individual outdoor-appliance line."
- "Electrical conduit depth depends on the wiring method — 24 inches for direct-buried cable, 18 inches for PVC conduit, 6 inches for rigid metal conduit, per NEC Table 300.5."
