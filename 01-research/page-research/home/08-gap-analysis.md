# Content Gap Analysis — D&D Land Works Homepage

Adapted from the parent methodology's 20-point HVAC gap framework, substituted for excavation/Oregon equivalents. Each gap is scored on:
- **Coverage in competitors** (0 = none of the 8 address it / 10 = all 8 address it)
- **Difficulty for D&D to fill** (1 = easy, have the info already / 5 = requires new asset, photography, or David's direct input)
- **Conversion impact** (1 = SEO/trust only / 5 = directly drives calls)

Ordered by `(10 - coverage) × conversion_impact / difficulty` — higher score = higher-leverage gap to fill first.

## Top 5 gaps to prioritize (by score, achievable within a first content pass — excludes the photo/review/city-page gaps that require time or new assets rather than just better copy)

1. **Bundled-service / one-contractor positioning** (score 40.0) — costs nothing to fix, directly matches David's own "Full-Service Excavation Company" line
2. **License-verification link** (score 36.0) — a single hyperlink to search.ccb.state.or.us
3. **Timeline specificity** (score 32.0) — cite the real ranges already sourced in this pack
4. **Soil-type / Willamette Valley clay callout** (score 30.0) and **Rainy-season scheduling guidance** (score 40.0 — tie for top) — both are zero-cost, zero-competitor-coverage, genuinely Oregon-specific
5. **Brand-scope-honesty statement** (score 30.0) — explicitly listing what D&D does NOT do, turning narrow scope into a trust signal

## Gaps that require David's input before they can be filled (flag explicitly — do not fabricate)

- Years in business / founding year
- Specific equipment/fleet brands
- Insurance coverage dollar amounts
- Typical response/scheduling turnaround
- Exact DEQ certification name/number
