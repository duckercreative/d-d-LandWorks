## 5-8 Customer Scenarios (Eugene-Specific, for Body Copy Framing)

These scenarios translate keyword intent into real customer situations. Each should inform how the body copy speaks to the reader.

**Scenario 1 — The Saturated Lawn**  
A homeowner in west Eugene (Bethel or Barger neighborhood) has Malpass clay under a lawn that pools every December. They had the yard regraded two years ago. Still wet. They've searched "French drain Eugene OR" and want to understand: is this the right solution, what does it involve, and what does it cost?

**Scenario 2 — The Crawl Space Concern**  
A homeowner in Springfield (older Ranch-style house) has a crawl space that gets damp every winter. They're not sure if they need a French drain around the foundation, a different kind of drain, or if it's actually a grading problem. They're searching "French drain around foundation Eugene" or "crawl space moisture drain Lane County."

**Scenario 3 — The Driveway Culvert**  
A rural Lane County property owner has a culvert under their gravel driveway that keeps washing out. They're searching "culvert installation Lane County" or "drainage contractor rural Lane County." They need someone who can dig, place a culvert pipe, and grade out the approach — scope that crosses drainage and driveway repair.

**Scenario 4 — The Storm-Drain Tie-In Question**  
A homeowner in central Eugene wants to tie their French drain into the city's stormwater system. They've been told they need a permit but no contractor they've called can explain exactly what that involves or who can do it. They're specifically searching because other contractors have been vague.

**Scenario 5 — The Contractor Referral**  
A grading/landscaping contractor told a homeowner they need a French drain and referred them to an excavation contractor. This homeowner is now searching for a specific contractor to do the buried-system work and wants to understand what the install actually involves so they can ask informed questions.

**Scenario 6 — The Pre-Project Homeowner**  
A homeowner is planning an ADU, fence, or patio project but has a drainage problem that keeps delaying it. They need the drainage solved first. They're likely also looking at the foundation-excavation or grading pages and may land on this page because their grading contractor said the yard needs a French drain before the project can start.
