## Competitive Intelligence Summary

**The structural gap in this market is unique:** even the two competitors who name "French drain" specifically (M4 Landworks, Pacific Earthworks) provide no depth, no pricing, no permit content, no material specs, no soil context, and no schema. This is not a "thin on one dimension" situation — it's a genuine content vacuum below the "names the service" level.

**D&D's positioning opportunity:** Be the first contractor in Lane County to explain *how* drainage installation actually works in Eugene's specific conditions (clay soil, wet season, permit requirement). Every technical spec — depth, slope, pipe type, filter fabric in clay soil, EC §6.610 permit restriction, "where does the water go" — is uncontested territory.

**Primary displacement targets (in order):**
1. Cojo Asphalt blog posts (out-of-area; full technical match + local authority beat)
2. M4 Landworks drainage page (local competitor; only one naming French drains)
3. Generic directory pages (Yelp, Angi) that aggregate contractors without content

**The absence of competitors with real drainage content means this page's primary ranking challenge is indexing, trust signals, and GBP presence — not out-competing superior content.**
