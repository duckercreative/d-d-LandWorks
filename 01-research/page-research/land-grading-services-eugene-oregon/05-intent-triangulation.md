## Customer Scenarios (Pain-to-Purchase Journey)

### Scenario 1: New Lot Buyer — Building First Home
- **Who:** Young couple buys 2-acre lot in Junction City or Creswell
- **Pain:** Raw land with no utilities or grade; builder says they need a building pad and rough grade before permits
- **Search:** "building pad grading Lane County Oregon" / "site prep contractor Eugene OR"
- **Decision:** Finds D&D, sees CCB#, DEQ cert, free estimate, calls same day
- **What they need to see:** Building pad services, permit knowledge, CCB license, fast response

### Scenario 2: Soggy Yard Homeowner — Drainage Crisis After Rain
- **Who:** Eugene homeowner; water pools next to foundation every winter
- **Pain:** Crawlspace moisture; soggy back lawn; Malpass clay doesn't drain
- **Search:** "drainage grading Eugene Oregon" / "yard drainage fix Eugene OR"
- **Decision:** Finds D&D, sees drainage grading services, reads Malpass clay explanation, calls
- **What they need to see:** Drainage grading services, French drain context, local soil knowledge

### Scenario 3: Gravel Driveway — Annual Rain Damage
- **Who:** Rural Lane County property owner (Veneta, Lowell area)
- **Pain:** Gravel driveway rutted and potholed after wet season; puddles forming on edges
- **Search:** "gravel driveway grading Eugene Oregon" / "driveway repair grading Lane County"
- **Decision:** Finds D&D, sees driveway grading callout, requests free estimate
- **What they need to see:** Driveway grading services, experience with gravel surfaces, service area confirmation

### Scenario 4: Pre-Permit Prep — Commercial Developer
- **Who:** Small developer planning a duplex or ADU in Eugene
- **Pain:** Architect flagged grade issue; needs grading before building permit approval
- **Search:** "site grading contractor Eugene OR" / "building pad grading Eugene"
- **Decision:** Needs commercial-capable contractor with permit knowledge and CCB license
- **What they need to see:** Commercial grading capability, CCB#, permit awareness, professional communications

### Scenario 5: Slope/Erosion Problem — Rural Hillside
- **Who:** Homeowner on sloped lot near Cottage Grove or west Eugene foothills
- **Pain:** Bank eroding after clear-cut or heavy rain; losing yard to slip
- **Search:** "slope stabilization grading Eugene OR" / "erosion control grading Lane County"
- **Decision:** Needs slope expert, erosion control knowledge, equipment for steep terrain
- **What they need to see:** Slope grading capability, erosion control experience, equipment references
