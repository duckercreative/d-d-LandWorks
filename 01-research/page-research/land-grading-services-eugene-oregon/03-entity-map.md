## Technical / Trade Entities (Concepts)

| Entity | Concept Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rough grade | Technical process step | Bulk earthmoving; sets building pad elevation, lot falls — comes before utilities and foundation; tolerance ±0.1 ft |
| Finish grade / Final grade | Technical process step | Last grading step before planting/paving; precision to ±0.5 in; shapes swales, directs runoff |
| Building pad | Construction feature | Leveled foundation platform at engineered elevation; required before any structure |
| Cut and fill grading | Technical method | Excavating high spots (cut) and using material to raise low spots (fill); balances material on-site to reduce haul costs |
| French drain | Drainage system | Perforated pipe in gravel trench; used in drainage grading to redirect subsurface water |
| Drainage swale | Drainage feature | Graded shallow channel to direct surface water away from structures |
| Topsoil stripping | Process step | Organic topsoil removed before structural fill; cannot remain under pads due to decomposition risk |
| Slope ratio | Technical standard | Cut/fill slope steeper than 2:1 triggers Lane County grading permit |
| Erosion control / EPSC | Regulatory requirement | Required during Oregon wet season (Oct–Apr); silt fence, cover, mulch, compost, or plastic sheeting |
| Site grading | Broad service term | General land shaping for drainage or construction; synonym for lot grading at project scale |
| Lot leveling | Consumer-facing term | Popular search term for residential yard/lot grading |
| Yard resloping | Consumer-facing term | Correcting existing grade to fix drainage |
