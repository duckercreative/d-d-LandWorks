**Analysis Framework for Each Competitor:**

**1. GBP & Local Pack**
- Reviews: count, rating, response rate, velocity (reviews/month)
- Categories: primary + secondary
- GBP post frequency and types
- Photos count
- Service menu completeness
- Review reply rate (GBP Q&A was removed by Google in late 2025 — don't audit for it)

**2. Website SEO**
- WebFetch their homepage: title tag, meta, H1, schema types
- Service page structure (how many service pages, how deep?)
- Location page strategy (how many, what format?)
- Blog/content volume and frequency
- Internal linking structure

**3. Keyword Positioning**
- What keywords do they rank for that we don't?
- What are their top 10 ranking pages (by estimated traffic)?
- Keyword gaps: they rank, we don't

**4. Content Strategy**
- Content depth (word count on key pages)
- E-E-A-T signals (team pages, certifications, awards)
- FAQ coverage
- AI citability of their content

**5. Backlink Profile**
- Estimated referring domains (from available tools)
- Key link sources (local press, industry associations, directories)
- Link acquisition strategy (sponsorships, blog outreach, citations)

**6. Positioning & Differentiation**
- Their main USPs (what do they lead with?)
- Pricing signals (do they show prices?)
- Offers / promotions
- Brand personality
