# Seasonal Houston — HVAC Demand & Post Themes

Month-by-month Houston HVAC context for Phase 2 decisions. Use to pick the right angle for the time of year when live weather data isn't available or to sanity-check weather-driven hooks.

Houston climate: humid subtropical, two long shoulder seasons, brutal summer, mild-but-occasionally-freezing winter. Demand pattern is driven by AC, not heating — HVAC revenue curve is heavily weighted toward April–October.

---

## Season key (maps to `post_templates.json` top-level keys)

| Season key | Months | Primary service focus | Typical weather |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pre_summer` | Mar 15 – May 31 | AC tune-up, filter, thermostat, duct check | 65–90°F, shoulder season, first load tests |
| `summer` | Jun 1 – Sep 15 | Emergency repair, 24/7, financing on replacements | 90–100°F, heat indexes 100–110+, thunderstorms |
| `fall` | Sep 16 – Nov 30 | Furnace tune-up, heating inspection, thermostat swap | 60–85°F, first cold fronts, freeze prep |
| `winter` | Dec 1 – Feb 28 | Heating emergency, furnace repair, freeze response, IAQ | 35–65°F, occasional 20s, rare freezes |
| `year_round` | any | Brand/trust/financing/reviews | any |

Use the active season to filter templates and set the narrative frame. Cross-season posts (e.g., promoting AC tune-up in August) feel off.

## Month-by-month

### January — Heating emergency + IAQ

**Demand driver:** Cold fronts (20s–30s), cedar allergy season kicks in, indoor air quality concerns from closed-house HVAC.

**Post themes:**
- Furnace won't ignite / short-cycling
- Heat pump defrost mode problems
- Emergency heating repair 24/7
- Air purifier + UV light installations
- Duct cleaning (winter is easier than summer)

**Keyword focus:** `furnace repair houston`, `emergency heating houston`, `heat pump repair houston`

---

### February — Late winter + early pre-summer seed

**Demand driver:** Last cold fronts, homeowners starting to think about AC. Tax refund season → bigger-ticket installs/replacements.

**Post themes:**
- Final furnace tune-up before spring
- "Your AC is next — here's what to check before summer"
- Replacement financing before spring rush
- IAQ maintenance plans

**Keyword focus:** `furnace repair houston`, `hvac maintenance plan houston`, `ac replacement houston`

---

### March — Pre-summer launch

**Demand driver:** First 85°F days. Homeowners realize AC didn't run well last summer. Peak window for tune-up conversions (easy to book, easy to sell).

**Post themes:**
- AC tune-up before summer rush
- Filter replacement
- Smart thermostat upgrade
- Refrigerant leak check
- Duct sealing (before summer AC load)

**Keyword focus:** `ac tune up houston`, `ac repair houston`, `smart thermostat install houston`

---

### April — Pre-summer peak for tune-ups

**Demand driver:** Temps hit 80s consistently. Humidity starts climbing. Systems tested — weak ones start failing.

**Post themes:**
- $89 AC tune-up (the classic Houston HVAC pre-summer play)
- Early warning signs your AC won't survive summer
- Refrigerant leaks caught now vs. in July
- Neighborhood geo posts — every suburb gets one this month
- Commercial HVAC inspections before high season

**Keyword focus:** `ac tune up houston`, `ac repair houston`, `ac repair <suburb> tx`

---

### May — Shoulder to peak

**Demand driver:** First genuine heat (mid-90s). Emergency calls start ticking up. Tune-up window closing — message shifts to "book before it's too late" then to repair/emergency.

**Post themes (first half):**
- Last-chance tune-up
- Duct sealing math ("20–30% cool air loss")
- Attic insulation + AC combo

**Post themes (second half):**
- Warning signs (warm air, strange noise, ice)
- 24/7 emergency framing begins
- Replacement financing for aging systems

**Keyword focus:** `ac repair houston`, `emergency ac repair houston`, `ac replacement houston`

---

### June — Emergency season opens

**Demand driver:** Real heat (95°F+, heat index 100+). AC failures on hottest days. Maximum call volume starts.

**Post themes:**
- 24/7 emergency AC repair
- Same-day service emphasis
- "Most repairs done on first visit"
- Storm/rain aftermath — flooded outdoor units
- Commercial emergency response

**Keyword focus:** `emergency ac repair houston`, `ac repair houston`, `24 hour ac repair houston`

---

### July — Peak emergency

**Demand driver:** Heat index regularly 105+. Hottest week of the year. Compressor failures peak. Customers desperate — conversions fast.

**Post themes:**
- Night + weekend emergency availability
- "Your AC is running constantly — here's what's happening"
- Replacement financing (when repair cost > 50% of replacement)
- Neighborhood deep-focus posts (most competitive search month)
- Ductless mini-split as quick-install alternative

**Keyword focus:** `emergency ac repair houston`, `ac replacement houston`, `hvac installation houston`

---

### August — Sustained emergency + replacement push

**Demand driver:** Heat holds, customers at wit's end with old systems. Replacement window opens. Back-to-school season — families want AC fixed before school starts.

**Post themes:**
- Replacement ROI (before the summer utility bills peak)
- Financing deep-dive
- Smart thermostat installs during existing service calls
- Commercial HVAC emergency (restaurants, retail)
- Heat pump as energy saver for next summer

**Keyword focus:** `ac installation houston`, `ac replacement houston`, `emergency ac repair houston`

---

### September — Emergency tapering + early heating seed

**Demand driver:** First half still summer, back half first cool fronts. Some customers start thinking about heating. Insurance claim season post-summer storms.

**Post themes:**
- Post-hurricane / post-storm AC inspection
- "Don't wait until winter to find out your heat doesn't work"
- Maintenance plan sign-ups (catches both AC end-of-season and heating prep)
- Commercial pre-heating season inspection

**Keyword focus:** `hvac maintenance houston`, `furnace inspection houston`, `heat pump repair houston`

---

### October — Fall transition

**Demand driver:** First genuine cold snaps. Heating systems get turned on for the first time in 6 months — weak ones fail. Freeze prep conversations start.

**Post themes:**
- Furnace tune-up before first cold front
- Heat pump reversing-valve check
- Carbon monoxide detector reminder (gas furnace safety)
- Air duct cleaning (fall is the best time)
- IAQ — air purifiers for winter closed-window season

**Keyword focus:** `furnace repair houston`, `heating tune up houston`, `hvac maintenance houston`

---

### November — Pre-winter prep

**Demand driver:** Cold fronts real and regular. First freeze watches possible. Customers nesting for winter.

**Post themes:**
- "Is your furnace ready for December?"
- Heat pump efficiency (Houston climate is perfect for heat pumps)
- Maintenance plan sign-ups
- IAQ — humidifiers for dry heated air
- Commercial heating inspections

**Keyword focus:** `furnace repair houston`, `heat pump installation houston`, `heating service houston`

---

### December — Winter + holiday

**Demand driver:** Occasional real cold (30s-40s, rare 20s). Gift/tax-year-end installs. Freeze warnings pop up.

**Post themes:**
- Emergency heating 24/7 (even Christmas)
- Freeze prep — pipe protection for heat pumps
- Year-end install for next year's efficiency + financing
- "No heat on Christmas morning" — framed emergency positioning
- IAQ — keeping winter air clean

**Keyword focus:** `emergency heating houston`, `furnace repair houston`, `heat pump installation houston`

## Weather-to-angle quick map

| Current condition | Post angle |
|---|---|
| Heat advisory / heat index 100+ | Emergency repair, 24/7, same-day |
| 85–95°F, no advisory | "Before peak hits" tune-up, refrigerant check |
| 70–85°F shoulder | Tune-up, thermostat upgrade, duct sealing |
| 55–70°F | Transition — maintenance plan, IAQ |
| Cold front incoming | Furnace tune-up, heat pump check, freeze prep |
| Freeze warning | Emergency heating, pipe protection, heat pump defrost |
| Major storm / hurricane aftermath | Outdoor unit inspection, flood-damaged systems |
| First 80°F day of year | "Your AC just worked for the first time — did it sound right?" |
