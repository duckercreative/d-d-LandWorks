## Post Title
HVAC Maintenance Spring TX — Are Your Ducts Cooling the Attic?

## Post Body
💨 HVAC maintenance in Spring, TX starts with a question: how much of your cool air is actually making it to the rooms? In most North Houston homes the honest answer is 70–80%.

Spring attic temps are already climbing into the 110–120°F range on 86°F afternoons. Every loose joint, torn flex duct, and unsealed plenum connection up there leaks conditioned air straight into that oven — and the closer we get to June, the more obvious the loss becomes.

Here's what a duct inspection catches in Spring, Klein, and the north Houston suburbs:
— Disconnected boots at supply registers (common after roof work or attic storage)
— Flex duct sagging below insulation lines, kinking airflow
— Mastic failure at the air handler's supply plenum
— Return-side leaks pulling hot attic air into the system
— R-value loss on old insulated trunk lines

The math is blunt: 20–30% duct loss means you're paying a 25% surcharge on every summer electric bill until it's fixed. Sealing and re-insulating typically pays back within a single Houston cooling season.
