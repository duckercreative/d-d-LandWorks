## Post Body
🌡️ AC repair in Cypress, TX — if your system is already running longer than it did last April, a slow refrigerant leak is one of the first things we'd check.

Cypress hit 86°F today and the shoulder season is officially over. This is the week weak systems start showing themselves — and refrigerant leaks that sat quiet all winter are at the top of the list. Your AC doesn't consume refrigerant, so if the charge is low, there's a leak somewhere in the line set, evaporator coil, or condenser.

Here's what we see on pre-summer diagnostic calls in Cypress and northwest Houston:
— Runs that take 20–30% longer to hit the setpoint
— Ice forming on the suction line or evaporator coil
— A faint hiss near the indoor unit or at the service valves
— Warm air from vents in the afternoon but cool at night
— Electric bills up noticeably vs. last April

Ignoring a leak wrecks the compressor — the single most expensive part in the whole system. Catching it now means a recharge plus a sealed leak, not a full replacement in July.
