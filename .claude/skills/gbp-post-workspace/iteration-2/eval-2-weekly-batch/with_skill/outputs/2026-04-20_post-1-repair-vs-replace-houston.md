## Post Title
AC Repair Houston — Know When to Fix vs. Replace

## Post Body
🌡️ AC repair in Houston gets expensive fast when you're fixing the wrong system.

The honest breakdown our techs give on every call: take the repair cost and multiply by the unit age in years. If that number clears $5,000, replacement usually pencils out better. A $700 compressor repair on a 9-year-old system hits $6,300 on that scale. A new 16 SEER2 unit pays that back inside 3 Houston summers.

Not every older unit needs to go. If the system is under 8 years old, well-maintained, and the repair is under $500, fix it. Houston heat is hard on equipment but a serviced unit with clean coils and a fresh capacitor can run another 5-7 years without drama.

What actually tells us a system is done:
- Refrigerant leaks that keep coming back (the leak is never in one spot)
- Compressor drawing high amps but barely cooling
- R-22 systems (refrigerant discontinued, parts getting scarce and expensive)
- Second major repair in 24 months on the same unit
