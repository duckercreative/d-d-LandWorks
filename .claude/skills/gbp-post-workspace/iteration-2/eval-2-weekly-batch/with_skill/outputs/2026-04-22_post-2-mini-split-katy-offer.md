AC installation in Katy, TX is booking up fast and April is the last easy week to get on the schedule.

Hot room problem in Cinco Ranch, Kelliwood, or Firethorne? This is worth fixing before June. Bonus rooms, converted garages, and upstairs bedrooms are the usual culprits. Central AC loses most of its punch before it reaches the far end of the duct run. By July, a third-floor bonus room can run 5-8 degrees warmer than the thermostat downstairs.

A ductless mini-split handles this differently. The wall-mounted head unit sits in the problem room and cools it directly. No duct extension. No competing with the rest of the house for airflow. Installation in a single room typically runs one day.

Here's what the install includes:
- Wall-mounted indoor air handler
- Outdoor compressor unit (added to existing pad or new pad installed)
- Line set run through exterior wall
- Full commissioning, charge check, and airflow verification
