HVAC Contractor Sugar Land TX — April Warning Signs

## Post Body
HVAC contractor Sugar Land TX homeowners call most in late April: systems that were fine all winter are now running 6-8 hours a day in the first real heat push, and the weak ones start showing signs.

Here is what to watch for in First Colony, Riverstone, and Telfair homes right now:

Your system starts cooling but takes 45+ minutes to pull the temperature down 2 degrees. That is either a refrigerant charge issue or coil fouling from a winter of sitting. Both are cheap to fix in April. Both become expensive in June if you wait.

The outdoor unit runs but the air from vents is only lukewarm. Check the filter first. If it was last changed in October it is probably blocking airflow. A clogged filter in an otherwise healthy system will make it act like it is failing.

Your electricity bill was noticeably higher than last April. A 15% jump with no lifestyle change usually points to a dirty condenser coil, low charge, or a degraded capacitor making the compressor work harder than it should.
