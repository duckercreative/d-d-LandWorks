## Post Body
🌿 AC repair in Cypress, TX often starts the same way in May: a drain line clogged with Cy-Fair pollen and algae.

This week is the shoulder window most homeowners miss. Highs in the mid-80s, systems running a few hours a day, nothing critical yet. That changes once June hits.

What we're finding on pre-summer calls in Cypress right now:

- Condensate drain lines packed with spring pollen. Bridgeland and Cypress Creek corridors see the worst of it from the tree canopy and standing moisture.
- Evaporator coils with a layer of cottonwood fluff cutting airflow by 10-15%. You won't notice today. You will at 97 degrees.
- Capacitors reading inside spec but running weak. Fine now. A week of July heat is a different story.
- Drain pan rust on 8-12 year systems. Common in Fairfield and Blackhorse Ranch homes from the early 2000s.

The drain line is the most common call. When it clogs, condensate backs up into the overflow pan. The float switch is supposed to shut the system off before it becomes a leak. About half the time it does. A tune-up visit catches it for next to nothing. A water damage call later does not.
