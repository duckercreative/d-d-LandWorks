# Voice Search Optimization

## The 3 Voice Pipelines (2026)

Voice searches do not all go through Google. Each assistant has its own data pipeline — optimize each independently:

| Assistant | Primary Source | What 75 Degree AC Must Have |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Assistant** | Featured snippets + GBP + Knowledge Graph | Complete GBP, FAQ schema, answer-first content |
| **Siri (Apple Intelligence)** | Apple Maps Connect + Apple Intelligence context | **Verified Apple Maps Connect listing** (currently missing — flag) |
| **Alexa** | **Bing index, not Google** | Verified Bing Places listing + Bingbot allowed |

For local intent ("near me", "open now"), 58% of voice queries are local — these route directly through the maps platform of the assistant, not through web search.

## Speakable Schema — Reality Check

Speakable schema was originally designed for news publishers and is not confirmed to affect local service voice results. **Keep it as an enhancement but de-prioritize against GBP/Apple Maps/Bing Places completeness.** GBP completeness has a more direct, confirmed path to voice answers than Speakable on a service page.

## Target Voice Queries

```
"OK Google, find AC repair near me"
"Hey Siri, who does 24/7 AC repair in Houston?"
"Alexa, is 75 Degree AC open right now?"
"Hey Google, how much does AC repair cost in Houston?"
```

## Voice Optimization Checklist

### Tier 1 (highest impact for local voice):
- [ ] GBP fully optimized (hours, services, attributes, photos)
- [ ] Apple Maps Connect listing claimed and complete (Siri pipeline)
- [ ] Bing Places listing verified (Alexa pipeline)
- [ ] Business hours in schema match GBP exactly
- [ ] Phone number in schema, GBP, Apple Maps, Bing Places — all identical

### Tier 2 (content-side):
- [ ] FAQ schema on all answer sections
- [ ] Short direct answer paragraphs (<30 words for voice readability)
- [ ] "near me" variants in content naturally
- [ ] Speakable schema on service descriptions (enhancement only)
