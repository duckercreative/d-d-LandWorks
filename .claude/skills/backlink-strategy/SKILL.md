# Backlink Strategy — 75 Degree AC

## Link Building Principles for Local HVAC

**Quality > Quantity.** 10 links from Houston-relevant, authoritative sites beat 100 generic directory links.

**Priority order:**
1. **AI-citation-source domains** (Wikipedia, Reddit threads, YouTube, Forbes, Business Insider, Houston Chronicle, niche B2B authorities, manufacturer dealer locators) — single strongest predictor of AI citations
2. Local Houston links (highest local pack ranking impact)
3. Industry association links (topical authority)
4. Manufacturer/brand directories (entity authority + contextual citation lift)
5. Home services platforms (traffic + sameAs citations)
6. Content/blogger outreach (topical authority)

**2026 AI citation + local SEO link data (validated 2026-05-22):**
- **Referring domains is the single strongest predictor of AI citations** (Decoding study). Sites with 2,500 referring domains average **1.6–1.8 AI citations** per query. Sites with 350,000 referring domains average **8.4 citations**. The relationship is roughly logarithmic — every 10× growth in referring domain count adds ~2 citations on average.
- **Citation quality > quantity** (BrightLocal 2026). 10 high-DR contextual backlinks from citation-relevant domains beat 50 inconsistent low-quality citations.
- Link signals = ~11% of local pack ranking weight (Whitespark 2024)
- Local + relevant links > authority-only links for local SEO
- Sponsored/community links count for local signal even if nofollowed
- **Manufacturer dealer-locator backlinks** (carrier.com, trane.com, lennox.com) carry both authority signal AND on-page authorization claim — pursue alongside content links
- **AI-citation source backlinks** are weighted differently from generic SEO backlinks — see Tier 0 below

## Link Tier 0: AI-Citation Source Domains (HIGHEST PRIORITY — added 2026-05-22)

Per 5W AI Platform Citation Source Index 2026 (680M citations analyzed), these domains drive disproportionate AI citation pull-through. Every backlink from these is worth multiples of a generic directory link.

| Target | Citation share | Pitch Angle | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wikipedia** | 26-48% of ChatGPT top-10 | Edit existing Houston HVAC / 25C tax credit / SEER2 articles to cite our research/data | Earned via genuine source-grade content; entity notability gated |
| **Wikidata Q-ID for 75 Degree AC** | Truth Node for Google KG + ChatGPT training | Create or claim Wikidata entry with sameAs, foundingDate, address, knowsAbout HVAC | wikidata.org/wiki/Special:NewItem |
| **Reddit comments with disclosure** | ~40% of all AI citations | Per `reddit-engagement` skill — every high-quality disclosed brand-mention comment is a potential citation source | Via reddit-engagement pipeline (not direct outreach) |
| **YouTube video descriptions** | #1 most-cited AI domain | Per `topical-map` skill Cluster 10 — our own YouTube content, plus appearances on Houston-HVAC creator channels | Build our own channel + collab outreach |
| **Forbes / Business Insider HARO/Featured.com** | Top ChatGPT weight | Expert quote pitches via Featured.com or direct journalist outreach | featured.com + journalist Twitter |
| **Houston Chronicle editorial** | Top Gemini/ChatGPT local weight | Expert source for HVAC stories | Reporter direct contact |
| **CenterPoint Energy rebate page** | Authoritative source AI engines cite | Contractor program listing | centerpointenergy.com contractor application |
| **TDLR contractor lookup** | Government authority | Active license verification listing — already in place but ensure data accuracy | tdlr.texas.gov |
| **NATE certification registry** | Industry authority | Technician profile listing (Kenny Ho) | natex.org |
| **EPA 608 registry** | Federal authority | Technician certification listing | epa.gov |
| **niche B2B HVAC authorities** (HVAC.com, achrnews.com, contractingbusiness.com) | Cited by Perplexity for technical questions | Expert quote contributions, original research data | Editor pitch |
| **Manufacturer dealer locators** (carrier.com, trane.com, lennox.com, goodman.com) | Direct authorization signal + contextual link | Become authorized — see Tier 3 | Manufacturer dealer rep |

**Monthly target:** 2-4 high-DR contextual backlinks from AI-citation-source domains. **De-prioritize:** generic directory submissions, link exchanges, low-DR business listings that don't move citations.

**Why this Tier 0 exists:** The validated 2026 research shows that AI citation likelihood scales with referring domain count from AI-citation-source domains specifically, not all backlinks equally. A Wikipedia citation moves the needle dramatically more than 50 generic Yellowpages-style directory listings.
