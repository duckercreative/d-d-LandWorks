## 3. Bidding Strategy — Phase 1 (Weeks 1–4, Zero Conversion Data)

Because you have zero conversion data, you are firmly in **Phase 1**.

### Phase 1 Strategy: **Maximize Conversions** — no tCPA target

**Hard rules during Phase 1:**
- Do **NOT** use Maximize Clicks — it optimizes for irrelevant clicks
- Do **NOT** set a tCPA target — you don't yet know the real market CPA, and a guessed-low tCPA puts the campaign in perpetual learning with no spend
- Do **NOT** set max CPC caps — let Google find the optimal bid
- Daily budget should be **at least 10× expected CPA**. With Houston Emergency CPL benchmarks of $45–$80, expect ~$60 CPA → $600/day ideal but $200/day works (slower learning)

You stay on Maximize Conversions until you accumulate **30+ conversions** in the campaign — that's the threshold to move to Phase 2.

### Phase 2 — Once You Hit 30+ Conversions (~Month 2)

**Strategy: Maximize Conversions + Target CPA**

- Set tCPA = Phase 1 average CPA + $10 buffer
  - Example: if Phase 1 averaged $58 CPL → set tCPA at **$68**
- Every 2 weeks: reduce tCPA by $5 until volume drops noticeably — that drop point is your floor

### Phase 3 — Month 3+ (60+ conversions/month sustained)

**Strategy: Target CPA (optimized) per campaign**, possibly Target ROAS if revenue attribution is wired up.

### tCPA Targets by Campaign (Phase 2 → Phase 3)

| Campaign | Phase 2 Start | Phase 3 Target |

## 5. Performance Max & LSA — Day One or Wait?

### Local Services Ads (LSA) — **Run from day one, in parallel with Search**

LSA appears **above** regular Search Ads. The Google Guarantee badge increases trust significantly. For HVAC specifically:
- Average LSA CPL: $25–$55 (often lower than Search)
- Pay per lead, not per click
- Occupies the position above your Search ads → you take **two slots** on page 1

**Setup requirements (do these in week 1 — verification takes time):**
- Google background check on technicians
- TACLA license verification (use **TACLA72152E**)
- Business insurance verification
- Google reviews — more reviews = better LSA ranking (this is why review velocity matters)

**LSA bidding:** Use **Maximize Leads** — manual LSA bidding rarely outperforms.

### Performance Max
The skill's account architecture is built around 5 Search campaigns plus LSA. Performance Max is **not** part of the launch architecture documented in this methodology. With zero conversion data, the bidding methodology is explicit: Phase 1 = Maximize Conversions on Search. Hold off on PMax until your Search campaigns are feeding the account real conversion data — there's no provision in the launch methodology to run PMax from day one.

**Bottom line:** LSA yes, day one. PMax — not part of the launch playbook; revisit after Phase 2 begins.

## 6. The 3 Mistakes Most HVAC Managers Make at Launch (avoid these)

From the methodology's "10 Common Mistakes" list, the three that hit hardest at launch:

### Mistake #1 — Setting tCPA too low at launch
Manager guesses "I want $40 CPL" and sets tCPA at $40 on day 1. Result: campaign sits in perpetual learning mode, almost no spend, no data ever accumulates.
**Fix:** Maximize Conversions (no tCPA) for the first 30 days. Only move to tCPA after 30+ conversions, and set it at observed CPA + $10.

### Mistake #2 — Search + Display mixed in the same campaign
Google's default "Search Network with Display Select" toggle dumps Display impressions into your Search campaign, dilutes conversion data, and tanks reported CTR/CVR.
**Fix:** Display Network **OFF**, Search Partners **OFF**, on every Search campaign — every time, no exceptions.

### Mistake #3 — Phone calls not tracked
HVAC customers **call**, they don't fill forms. If call tracking isn't wired up, your tCPA bidder has nothing to optimize toward — you'll burn $6,000/month optimizing for nothing.
**Fix:** CallRail + GTM with a 60-second minimum call duration as the conversion threshold, before the first ad goes live.

### Honorable mentions (also in the top 10 — don't do these either)
- Sending traffic to the homepage instead of a dedicated landing page per campaign (kills QS, raises CPC, drops CVR)
- Launching with broad match (ads serve for unrelated queries) — Exact + Phrase only for the first 60 days
- Equal budget across all 5 campaigns — always 40/25/20/10/5
- Putting Emergency and General Repair in the same campaign — bid compromise plus generic ad copy

## 8. Weekly Optimization Workflow (Every Monday — 55 minutes)

This is non-negotiable. Block it on the calendar.

| Step | Time | Action |
|------|------|--------|
| 1. Search Terms Audit | 20 min | Filter last 7 days. **Conversions = 0 AND Clicks > 3** → add as negative. **Conversions ≥ 1** → add as new exact-match keyword. |
| 2. Keyword Performance | 15 min | Sort by Cost desc. **Pause** any KW with Cost > $50 AND Conversions = 0. **Fix** any with CTR < 2% AND Impressions > 100 (rewrite ad or change match type). **Scale** any with Conversions ≥ 2 AND CPL < target. |
| 3. Ad Performance | 10 min | Combinations tab → flag "Low" headlines, replace. Any AG with CTR < 5% → test fresh headlines. No "Good"/"Excellent" RSA after 4 weeks → rewrite all 15 headlines. |
| 4. Budget Review | 5 min | IS lost to budget > 15% → raise budget or tighten targeting. IS lost to rank > 20% → improve QS or raise bids. |
| 5. Conversion Tracking Health | 5 min | Verify conversions are reasonable (0 = check tracking immediately). Cross-reference CallRail data — must match within 10%. |

### Scaling rules
- **Scale when:** IS < 60%, CVR > 10%, CPL below target
- **Don't scale when:** CPL above target, QS < 6, landing page converts poorly, search terms are polluted
- **Rule:** Never increase budget by more than 20% per week
