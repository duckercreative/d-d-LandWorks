# Review Strategy — 75 Degree AC

## Why Reviews Are Critical

- Review signals = **16–20% of local pack ranking weight** (Whitespark 2026)
- **Review velocity jumped from factor #93 to #11 in 2026** — recency now outweighs total count. 5 fresh reviews in the last 30 days beats 200 stale reviews.
- **Owner response rate of 80%+** triggers a measurable ranking lift (Google has confirmed responding to reviews improves rank)
- Star rating displayed in SERP = direct CTR impact
- **AggregateRating schema** enables star snippets in organic results
- AI systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity) cite review platforms as authority signals
- **Review keywords feed RELEVANCE**, not just prominence. Reviews that mention specific services ("emergency AC repair"), neighborhoods ("Katy"), or products ("mini-split") reinforce ranking for those exact query types.

**Houston HVAC Benchmarks:**
- Top local pack position: 200+ Google reviews, 4.8+ rating, AND fresh reviews in last 30 days
- Winning vs. established competitors requires velocity + recency + response rate + keyword-rich review text
- Review recency weighted 3–5× more than older reviews (last 60–90 days most heavily weighted post-March 2026 core update)
- A profile with 80 reviews + steady weekly flow now outranks one with 200 reviews and nothing in the past 6 months

**Targets:**
- Month 3: 60+ reviews, 4.8+ avg, 80%+ response rate
- Month 6: 120+ reviews, 4.9+ avg, 80%+ response rate
- Velocity: 8–15 new reviews/month (consistent weekly cadence — not batched)
- Response time: within 24 hours for ALL reviews (positive and negative)

**Solicitation tactics that lift keyword relevance:**
Don't just ask for "a review" — ask a question that plants a keyword. After job completion, text: "What did [Tech Name] fix today?" or "How was your emergency AC repair experience in [neighborhood]?" The reviewer's answer naturally contains the query terms Google uses for relevance scoring. Never script or dictate review text (Google's review-spam detection flags coordinated patterns).

## Cross-Platform Review Sync (added 2026-05-22 — AI citation validated)

AI engines cross-validate review consistency across platforms — a 4.8 on Google + 4.7 on Yelp + 4.6 on Angi + A+ BBB is treated as more verifiable than 5.0 on Google alone. Cross-platform EEAT signals carry a Perplexity-specific weighting (per validation research).

**Synchronization workflow:**

1. **Birdeye hub** — set up Birdeye aggregator profile to pull Google + Yelp + Facebook reviews into one URL. Add to sameAs schema. Single canonical "review pool" link for AI to cite.

2. **Foursquare claim + sync** — claim Foursquare listing immediately. Verify hours, address, phone, website, photos match GBP exactly. Solicit ≥10 initial Foursquare reviews via post-job SMS variants.

3. **Service-specific solicitation templates** — rotate per service:
   - After AC repair: *"How was your AC repair? A 1-minute review on [platform] helps your Houston neighbors find us when their AC fails."*
   - After AC installation: *"Now that your new system is running, would you share your install experience on [platform]?"*
   - After furnace repair: *"How's your furnace running now? A quick review on [platform] helps another Houston homeowner."*
   - After ductless install: *"Got a minute to share your ductless install experience? [platform] link below."*
   - After emergency call: *"Glad we got there in time. If you've got 30 seconds, your review on [platform] helps the next emergency caller find us fast."*

4. **Neighborhood-level review targeting** — when soliciting reviews, request that the customer mention the neighborhood ("Katy", "Sugar Land", "Heights", "Memorial") if comfortable. Neighborhood-tagged reviews feed location-page social proof + AI geographic relevance.

5. **Platform rotation logic** — vary platform request per job (don't always default to Google):
   - 60% of post-job SMS → Google
   - 15% → Foursquare (priority growth)
   - 10% → Yelp
   - 10% → BBB
   - 5% → Facebook
   
   Track in dispatch records which platform was requested for which job. Avoid platform fatigue on any one customer.

6. **AggregateRating schema sync** — when monthly review tally moves, re-deploy AggregateRating schema with the updated count + rating per `schema-markup` skill. dateModified bump on home + service pages.

## Review Keyword Mining → Content Gap Analysis Loop (added 2026-05-22)

Customer reviews surface vocabulary and pain points that competitors miss. Mine them into content per this loop:

1. **Monthly review pull** — extract all 5-star reviews from past month.
2. **Phrase extraction** — list every concrete phrase (≥3 words) that includes: service name, neighborhood, problem description, brand name, technician name, time-of-day, weather context, emotional tone.
3. **Cross-reference with `02-question-mine.md`** — phrases not already covered become new FAQ candidates.
4. **Feed to `content-strategy`** — phrases informing Houston-specific content angles (e.g., "showed up during the storm", "found the capacitor in 10 minutes", "didn't try to upsell me").
5. **Feed to `topical-map`** — service × neighborhood combinations frequently mentioned become candidates for new neighborhood pages.
6. **Feed to `cro-strategy`** — phrases describing pre-call hesitation surface conversion friction to address on landing pages.

Output: `09-social-media/platforms/reviews/data/review-mining-[month].csv` with columns: phrase, frequency, service_tag, neighborhood_tag, content_destination.

### Negative Reviews (1–2 star)

```
[Name], we're sorry to hear your experience wasn't what we'd expect of ourselves. 
We stand behind our work and want to make this right. Please call us directly at 
(713) 598-2737 and ask for [Manager Name] — we'll take care of this for you. 
— 75 Degree AC Team
```

**Critical rules for negative responses:**
- Never be defensive
- Never argue with facts in the review
- Always offer to resolve by phone (not in public comment)
- Never offer discounts/refunds publicly (invites gaming)
- Respond within 24 hours maximum

## Review Content Mining

**What to look for in 5-star reviews:**
- Phrases customers use naturally → use in content + ad copy
- Specific technicians praised → highlight on About page
- Specific services praised → feature in content for those pages
- Urgency stories → use in emergency page messaging

**Example mining:**
Review: "They came out same day and fixed our AC in 2 hours — amazing"
→ Content: "Most repairs completed within 2 hours of arrival"
→ Ad copy: "Same-Day AC Repair — Fixed in Hours, Not Days"

## AggregateRating Schema

Once reviews are accumulating, add to homepage and service pages:
```json
{
  "@type": "AggregateRating",
  "ratingValue": "4.9",
  "reviewCount": "87",
  "bestRating": "5",
  "worstRating": "1"
}
```
**Update this monthly** as review count changes. Stale numbers = negative trust signal.
