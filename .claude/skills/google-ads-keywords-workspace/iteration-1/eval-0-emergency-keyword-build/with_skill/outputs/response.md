## 1. Campaign 1 Architecture — 4 Ad Groups

The skill maps Emergency Intent → Campaign 1, AGs 1/3/4, and Symptom Intent → Campaign 1, AG2. Four ad groups, intent-isolated, with cross-contamination negatives between them.

| Ad Group | Intent Theme | Match Type Mix (Launch) | Bid Posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AG1 — 24-Hour / Emergency** | "emergency", "24/7", "after hours", "weekend" | Phrase (primary) + Exact | Highest |
| **AG2 — Symptom** | "not cooling", "blowing warm", "leaking", "freezing up" | Phrase first | High |
| **AG3 — Broken / Not Working** | "broke down", "stopped working", "not working", "no ac" | Phrase + Exact | High |
| **AG4 — Same-Day / Today** | "same day", "today", "tonight", "now", "fix today" | Phrase + Exact | Highest |

Why 4 ad groups not 1: each cluster has a different urgency signal which should be reflected in headline pinning (handled in `google-ads-copy`). Mixing them forces a bid + ad-copy compromise. Bid that's right for "24 hour" overpays for "ac freezing up", and the headline that converts "ac not cooling" doesn't convert "no ac in house at midnight."

## 6. Brand Bidding Strategy — DO We Bid on "75 Degree AC"?

**YES. Bid on your own brand.** Direct quote of the skill rationale:

> Competitors will bid on your brand. If you don't show, they take your traffic at $1-2 CPC while you'd have got it at $0.10-0.30. Brand keyword QS is typically 10/10 because the LP-headline-keyword match is perfect, so the cost is trivial.

### Brand campaign setup (separate campaign, NOT inside the Emergency campaign)

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign type | Search, separate from Campaign 1–5 | Track brand vs generic conversions distinctly |
| Daily budget | $20–$30 cap | Rarely exhausts; sets a defensive ceiling |
| Match types | Phrase + Exact only — **NEVER Broad** | Broad would cannibalize generic non-brand queries |
| Bid strategy | Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks (low cap) | Volume is small; Smart Bidding doesn't get enough signal |
| Target QS | **10/10** — if not, brand LP is misconfigured | Skill's QS diagnostic table |

### What NOT to do on brand
- Do NOT add brand keywords to Campaign 1 (Emergency). They live in their own campaign so reporting stays clean and bid logic stays separate.
- Do NOT use Broad on brand. The skill is explicit: it cannibalizes generic non-brand searches and inflates brand CPL artificially.
- Do NOT exclude brand from your generic campaigns via account-level negatives. The brand campaign + tighter Exact match in brand will out-rank generic-campaign brand matches naturally; no need for a manual block.

### Competitor bidding (asked-adjacent — skill recommendation)
Skill says: **Selectively, not as a default.** Don't run a generic competitor campaign. Only bid competitor brands when you have a clear differentiator (e.g., "Same-day, no overtime fees" beats a competitor known for after-hours surcharges). If running, separate campaign, capped budget, ad copy must NOT name the competitor (Google policy violation).

For Emergency-campaign launch: **skip competitor bidding for now.** Revisit in Phase 2 (after 60+ conversions across the account) once you've identified competitors with weak after-hours coverage.

## 7. Geo Targeting (Houston + Katy + Sugar Land + Cypress + Richmond TX)

```

- Houston, TX (city)

- Richmond, VA
- Richmond, CA

```

**Location options:** Set "Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations" — NOT "Presence or interest." Interest targeting pulls in searchers who once mentioned Houston but live in Phoenix.

**Richmond TX safeguard layers** (the skill emphasizes this 3 times):
1. Account-level negatives: `richmond va`, `richmond virginia`, `virginia` (in Layer 1 above)
2. Geo include = Richmond TX only, geo exclude = Richmond VA/CA
3. Search Terms weekly review — flag any "richmond" query whose user location was outside TX

## 9. Week 1+ Maintenance Plan

**Monday — Search Terms triage (every Monday, no exceptions)** following the skill's decision tree:

1. Filter Search Terms → Last 7 Days, sort by Cost desc
2. For each term:
   - Converted? → Promote to Exact match (or Phrase if still Phase 1)
   - >$30 spend / >5 clicks / 0 conv / relevant? → Fix the ad/LP, don't negative yet
   - >$30 spend / >5 clicks / 0 conv / junk? → Add as negative (account-level if cross-campaign, otherwise campaign or AG)
   - Junk traffic (jobs/parts/DIY/out-of-area/warranty/school)? → Add as negative
   - <$30 spend, 0 conv, some clicks? → Leave it, re-evaluate next week

**Phase progression triggers** (per skill):
- 0–14 conv/30d → keep Phrase + Exact (current)
- 15–59 conv/30d → keep Phrase primary, promote proven Exact converters
- 60+ conv/30d → introduce *selective* Broad on best-performing themes, paired with Smart Bidding (tCPA or Max Conv)

**Quality Score audit (weekly):**
- Sort keywords by QS asc
- QS 1–3 → LP problem (page slow / not mobile / irrelevant)
- QS 4–5 → ad relevance (keyword not in any RSA headline of that ad group → fix in `google-ads-copy`)
- QS 6 → expected CTR (test stronger angle: urgency + social proof + price)
- Target QS for emergency keywords: **7+**. Anything 4 or below at 100+ impressions → pause and fix root cause.
