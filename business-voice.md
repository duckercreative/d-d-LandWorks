## Persona (the voice we speak as) — draft, confirm with David

An excavation contractor who runs the equipment himself, not a marketing department. Someone who's cleared land, cut a driveway, and put in a septic system on the same job site.

| Trait | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Stance** | Direct, practical, no-nonsense. Not salesy. Not corporate. |

## Sentence style

- Span sentence length at least 3× ratio per paragraph: shortest 5 words, longest 25+
- No run of 3+ sentences within 5 words of each other (burstiness rule)
- Vary openers — no two consecutive sentences start with the same word
- Fragments allowed and encouraged when they hit. "Grade the pad. Compact it. Done."
- Em dashes BANNED (project-wide rule, carried over)

## Predicate diversity rule (active verbs)

Rotate verbs describing the work. No verb may repeat within 3 sentences.

**Approved active verbs for excavation work:**

- **Site prep:** clear · grub · strip topsoil · prep · stake
- **Earthmoving:** excavate · grade · cut · fill · level · compact
- **Utility/drainage:** trench · bury · lay (pipe) · tie in · drain
- **Septic:** install · repair · inspect (per DEQ standards)
- **Operational:** dispatch · quote · schedule · complete · document

**Anti-pattern:** "We provide grading. We provide excavation. We provide site prep." → REWRITE: "We grade, excavate, and prep sites for residential and commercial builds."

## Salient triples rule

Every claim sentence should follow `[subject] [active predicate] [specific object]` with at least one verifiable element (number / named entity / location) once those specifics are known.

| Weak (banned) | Strong (approved, once specifics confirmed) |
|---|---|
| "We're reliable" | "D&D Land Works has completed [N] site prep jobs across [region]" |
| "Quality service" | "Licensed and bonded under CCB #261742, DEQ certified for septic work" |
| "Expert crew" | "[N] years running excavation equipment in [region]" |

## The 5-test gate (anti-AI · enforced by humanizer)

Every piece of copy must pass ALL five before shipping:

1. **Swap test** — Replace "D&D Land Works" + the service region with a competitor + another region. If the text still works unchanged, it's too generic. REWRITE.
2. **Read-aloud test** — Sounds like an excavation contractor talking, not a brochure.
3. **Burstiness test** — Sentence lengths span at least a 3× ratio (shortest 5w, longest 25w+).
4. **Specificity test** — Every paragraph has at least one concrete verifiable detail.
5. **Voice test** — At least one opinion / hedge / experiential take per ~300 words.

If any test fails, the FAILING SECTION gets rewritten — not patched.

**Self-audit second pass:** After humanization, ask "What still sounds AI?" Answer in 3-6 bullets. Rewrite SPECIFICALLY targeting those tells.
